About me

I help English speakers love speaking Hebrew

I'm a certified Hebrew teacher, trained at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem. For many years I have taught Hebrew to English speakers: new immigrants, retirees, parents and grandparents, and professionals preparing for licensing exams.

Over the years I developed a clear, proven method that gets adults speaking from the very first lesson. My students reach their goals: they hold real conversations, read the newspaper, work in Hebrew, and pass their exams.

The method

A proven method, step by step

Every course follows the same four steps. That's why my students see real progress within weeks.

  1. Placement

    A short conversation to find your exact level and your goal.

  2. Personal plan

    A lesson plan built around what you need: daily life, work, or an exam.

  3. Speak from day one

    Grammar explained clearly in English, then used right away in real Hebrew conversation.

  4. Measured progress

    Regular check-ins, so you always see how far you've come and what's next.

The path

Every level, all the way to the exam

Courses follow the familiar ulpan levels, so you always know where you stand and what comes next.

  1. אAleph · Beginner

    Reading and writing the alphabet, vowels, and everyday words.

    You'll: introduce yourself, shop, and read signs.

  2. בBet · Elementary

    Past and future tenses, verb groups (binyanim), and everyday conversation.

    You'll: chat with neighbours and handle the doctor or the bank in Hebrew.

  3. גGimel · Intermediate

    Reading without vowels, the news, and longer conversations.

    You'll: follow a news item and write a clear email.

  4. דDalet · Upper intermediate

    Formal language, opinions and arguments, and newspaper articles.

    You'll: take part in work meetings and handle official documents.

  5. הHey · Advanced

    Rich vocabulary, literature, and confident academic and professional writing.

    You'll: present in Hebrew and read professional texts.

  6. ✦Exam preparation

    Focused prep for Hebrew proficiency exams such as YAEL, and for the Hebrew requirement in professional licensing (medicine, law, nursing and more).

    You get: practice tests, timed reading, and the vocabulary of your field.

Use the level finder above, or start with a trial lesson. In that lesson I assess your level and recommend the right course.

By credit card through Grow, by Bit, or by bank transfer. After payment, message me on WhatsApp and we'll schedule your first lesson.

Which exams do you prepare for?

Hebrew proficiency exams such as YAEL, and the Hebrew requirement for professional licensing. Tell me your exam and I'll build a plan around its format.

Where do the lessons take place?

All lessons are online on Zoom, so you can learn from home, from anywhere in Israel or abroad. Before the first lesson I'll send you a link and make sure everything works.

Yes. Let me know at least 24 hours in advance and we'll find another time.
